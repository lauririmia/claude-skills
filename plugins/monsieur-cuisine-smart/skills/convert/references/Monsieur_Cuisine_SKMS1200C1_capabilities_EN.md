# 2. Using the mixing bowl

### Area of use

The mixing bowl **5** is suitable, for example, for mixing liquids, making smoothies, crushing ice cubes and nuts, and pureeing cooked or soft vegetables and fruit.

**NOTES:**

- This instruction manual describes the basic functions such as mixing, chopping and steaming.
- The food quantities given in the recipes refer to the quantities of the still unpeeled food.

## 2.1 Markings in the mixing bowl

The mixing bowl **5** has markings for the filling quantity on the inside. There are 6 markings from 0.5 litre (0.5L) to 3.0 litres MAX (3.0L MAX).

- Marking 3.0L: approx. 3 litres, corresponds to the maximum permitted filling quantity.

**CAUTION!**

When mixing liquids at level 10 or with the `TURBO` button, there must be a maximum of 2.5 litres in the mixing bowl **5**, otherwise liquid may be thrown out. At speed levels lower than 10, the mixing bowl** 5** may be filled up to the maximum of 3 litres.

**NOTE:** For some tasks there should not be too little liquid in the mixing bowl **5** so that the device can work properly. For example, when whipping cream you should use at least 200 ml, and when whisking egg whites at least 4.

## 2.2 Inserting and removing the blade unit

The blade unit **18** is used for chopping food. The mixing bowl **5** must never be used without the blade unit **18**, as the blade unit seals the bottom of the mixing bowl** 5**.

**DANGER of injury from cutting!**

Note that the blades of the blade unit **18** are very sharp. Never touch the blades with bare hands to avoid cuts.

To remove the blade unit **18**, proceed as follows:

1. Lay the mixing bowl **5** down horizontally.
2. Press the release lever **26** on the underside of the mixing bowl **5** so that it points to the symbol for the open lock.
3. Carefully pull the blade unit **18** upwards and out. To insert the blade unit **18**, proceed as follows:
1. Lay the mixing bowl **5** down horizontally.
2. Insert the blade unit **18** from above. Note that the two lugs at the base of the blade unit **18** must be pushed through the two recesses in the base of the mixing bowl **5**.

**CAUTION!**

Make sure that the seal **17** is seated correctly on the blade unit **18**, otherwise contents could leak out.

3. To lock the blade unit **18**, move the release lever** 26 **on the underside of the mixing bowl** 5** so that it points to the symbol for the closed lock.

**NOTE:** Cut large pieces into smaller ones with an edge length of about 3 - 4 cm before putting them into the mixing bowl. Larger pieces could otherwise get jammed in the blades.

## 2.3 Inserting the mixing bowl

1. Place the mixing bowl **5** in the seat **10**. The front with the handle faces you.
2. Press the mixing bowl **5** gently down until it is seated properly.

**NOTES:**

- If the mixing bowl **5** cannot be pressed down, you have to "wiggle" it a little so that the flower-shaped hub of the blade unit **18** in the mixing bowl **5** slides into the blade seat **11**.
- If the mixing bowl **5** is not inserted correctly, a safety mechanism prevents the device from working.

## 2.4 Inserting and removing the stirring attachment

The stirring attachment **20** is used for mixing thin liquid foods, e.g. for whipping cream, whisking egg whites, emulsifying (e.g. mayonnaise). The stirring attachment **20** is placed onto the blade unit **18**:

- The wings of the stirring attachment **20** are located in the gaps between the blades.
- For further notes: see section 1.2, Overview of functions.

**CAUTION!**

The stirring attachment **20** may only be used with speed levels 1 to 4.

The `TURBO` button must not be used when the stirring attachment **20** is used.

When using the stirring attachment **20**, the spatula** 21 **must not be used, as it could get into the stirring attachment** 20**.

When adding food, make sure that it does not block the stirring attachment **20**.

- To remove the stirring attachment **20**, pull it upwards. If it catches, turn it slightly.

## 2.5 Placing the lid on the mixing bowl

**NOTE:** The lid **23** for the mixing bowl may only be placed on once the mixing bowl **5** has been correctly inserted into the base unit **13**. If you put the lid on first, the mixing bowl can no longer be inserted into the base unit.

1. Place the lid **23** for the mixing bowl so that the lock symbols are opposite the arrows on the mixing bowl **5**.
2. Turn the lid **23** clockwise with both hands.

Only when the lid **23** is properly closed can the device start. If you are in a program, an audible signal indicates that the device is ready for operation.

## 2.6 Measuring cup as a lid for the filling opening

The measuring cup **24** serves at the same time as a closure for the mixing bowl **5** and for measuring ingredients (20 ml to 100 ml).

- To close the mixing bowl **5**, insert the measuring cup** 24 **into the lid** 23** with the opening facing down. Lock it by turning it clockwise. It clicks audibly into place.

**CAUTION!**

Before removing the measuring cup **24**, you must reduce the speed to level 1, 2 or 3 so that no food can be thrown out.

- To add ingredients, the measuring cup **24** can be briefly removed.
- To measure ingredients, turn the measuring cup **24** upside down and put the food in. The values can be read in ml.

## 2.7 Cooking and steaming with the cooking insert

1. Fill at least 500 ml of water into the mixing bowl **5**. Add no more water to the mixing bowl** 5** than will keep the maximum filling quantity of 3 litres from being exceeded when the food is added.
- If all the food is covered with water, it is boiled.
- If the food is not completely covered with water, it is steamed.
2. Put the food into the cooking insert **19**.

**NOTE:** Make sure that the fill level does not exceed the **MAX** marking.

3. Place the cooking insert **19** in the mixing bowl **5**.
4. Close the mixing bowl **5** with the lid **23** and the measuring cup **24**.
5. Switch the device on with the on/off switch **6**.
6. Select `MANUAL COOKING`.
7. Tap `TEMPERATURE` and set the desired temperature with the rotary knob **8**, e.g. 100 °C for boiling.
8. Tap `TIME` and set the desired time with the rotary knob **8**. The set time and the selected temperature are shown on the screen** 7**.
9. Select speed level *1* for an even distribution of heat in the water.
10. Press the `START` button. The device begins to work.
11. When the set time has elapsed, a signal sounds.
12. Press the on/off switch **6** to switch the device off.

## 2.8 Using the spatula

The spatula **21** is used for stirring ingredients and for removing the cooking insert **19**.

**CAUTION!**

The spatula **21** must not be used when the stirring attachment **20** is inserted. Otherwise the spatula **21** could get caught in the stirring attachment **20**.

Do not use any other kitchen tool to stir the ingredients. The tool could get into the blades and cause damage.

- Always stir **clockwise**. The spatula** 21 **is designed so that it cannot come into contact with a rotating blade unit** 18** when stirring clockwise.
- With the hook on the back of the spatula **21**, the hot cooking insert** 19 **can be removed without having to touch it directly. Simply hook the metal handle of the cooking insert** 19** and pull it upwards and out.

Notes on changing the tip and on cleaning (see "Cleaning, checking and replacing the spatula" in the original manual; not included in this file).

## 2.9 Removing the mixing bowl

**DANGER of injury from burns!**

If the contents of the mixing bowl **5** are hot, the lid **23** may only be opened slowly and carefully. Immediately after pureeing, the mixture in the mixing bowl is still moving and could gush out if the lid is opened too quickly. Therefore wait approx. 10 seconds after pureeing before you open the lid.

1. Turn the lid **23** counterclockwise with both hands and then take it off.
2. Lift the mixing bowl **5** upwards and out.

# 3. Operating the device

## 3.1 Setting the time

Here you set the desired time for the cooking process by scrolling in the time display.

Simply swipe your finger across the screen.

- The adjustable time varies depending on which program you have selected (see section 1.1, Overview of the programs).
- The set time is displayed on the screen **7**.
- When the set time has elapsed,
- the device stops,
- a beep sounds,
- the LED ring flashes green briefly and then lights up white.
- After the function starts, the time is counted down on the left of the screen and the set time is displayed below it.
- If no time was specified, the time display counts up from 0 seconds *(`00:00`)* to 99 minutes *(`99:00`)* and cannot be changed during operation. The device then stops.

You can also use the rotary knob **8** for the setting:

- turn clockwise: increase time
- turn counterclockwise: decrease time When using the rotary knob, the setting is made in the following steps:
- up to 1 min. in steps of seconds,
- from 1 min. to 5 min. in steps of 10 seconds,
- from 5 min. to 20 min. in steps of 30 seconds,
- from 20 min. to 99 min. in steps of minutes.
- If a time was set at the beginning, it can be changed during operation. Tap the `TIME` field and make the change. Confirm the change by tapping ` CONFIRM`.

## 3.2 Setting the temperature

Here you set the cooking temperature by swiping over the display control or by turning the rotary knob **8**:

- clockwise: increase temperature
- counterclockwise: decrease temperature
- The temperature can be changed during operation.
- Press the `TEMPERATURE` field and make the changes.
- Confirm your changes by tapping `CONFIRM` or discard them by tapping ` Cancel`.

### General information

- The adjustable temperature varies depending on which program you have selected (see section 1.1, Overview of the programs).
- The maximum temperature is 130 °C.
- The set temperature is displayed on the left of the screen. The actual temperature is displayed below it.
- To start the heating process, a speed level should be selected and a time set.
- During heating, the display of the food temperature may fluctuate considerably.

**NOTES:**

- As soon as you set a temperature, the speed is reset to level 3 if necessary.
- If you want to puree directly after cooking (e.g. a soup), first turn the temperature to `0` and then set the speed.

## 3.3 Pausing and ending

- To interrupt a running program, press:
- `PAUSE`: The time is no longer counted down.
- You can end the pause by pressing `RESUME`.
- To end a running program, press:
- `PAUSE` and then the ` STOP` button.

## 3.4 Setting the speed (LEVEL)

Here you set the speed *(`LEVEL`)* by turning the rotary knob **8**.

The speed can be set in 10 levels:

- Level 1 - 4: for stirring attachment **20**
- Level 1 - 10: for blade unit **18**

2 time ranges apply to the speed:

- Level 1 - 6: For these speeds the time can be selected from 1 second to 99 minutes.
- Level 7 - 10: For these speeds the time can be selected from 1 second to 10 minutes.

**CAUTION!**

The stirring attachment **20** may only be used with speed levels 1 to 4. A corresponding message appears on the screen **7**.

When mixing liquids at level 10, there must be a maximum of 2.5 litres in the mixing bowl **5**, otherwise liquid may be thrown out.

At speed levels lower than 10, the mixing bowl **5** may be filled up to the maximum of 3 litres.

- The set speed level is displayed on the left of the screen **7**.
- The speed level can be changed during operation.
- In some programs no speed can be selected, e.g. when steaming.
- If you change the speed to `0` during operation, the device stops. To continue working, make all settings again and press ` START`.
- If a temperature has been selected, only speed levels 1 - 3 are available.

### Soft start

To prevent liquid from hot food from being thrown out of the mixing bowl **5** or the device from shaking when accelerating suddenly, there is an automatic soft start for all settings except the Turbo function:

- Soft start is active at food temperatures from 60 °C.

## 3.5 Reverse run - changing the direction of rotation

The reverse run is used for gentle stirring of foods that are not to be chopped.

- By pressing the `DIRECTION OF ROTATION` button, the direction of rotation of the blade unit **18** can be changed at any time during normal operation.
- The reverse run can only be used at low speed - level 1 to 3.
- In the programs `KNEAD` and ` SEARING` the reverse run is controlled by the program.
1. Press `DIRECTION OF ROTATION`. The two directions of rotation are displayed on the right of the screen.
2. Select the respective direction of rotation by tapping `FORWARD` or ` REVERSE`. You can also make the setting with the rotary knob **8**.

## 3.6 Turbo function

**CAUTION!**

When mixing liquids, there must be a maximum of 2.5 litres in the mixing bowl **5**, otherwise liquid may be thrown out.

Never use this function when the stirring attachment **20** is in use.

With the Turbo function you can briefly select speed level *10* (maximum). This serves, for example, to quickly chop nuts, small amounts of herbs or onions.

1. Press the `TURBO` button. A new window appears.
2. Keep the rotary knob **8** pressed for as long as you need the function. After 20 seconds the function ends automatically.
3. To repeat, release the button and press it again.

**NOTES:**

- The Turbo function only works
- when the blade unit **18** is not rotating,
- when the temperature of the food in the mixing bowl **5** is below 60 °C.
- The speed is suddenly increased to level *10* (maximum).

## 3.7 Scale

The built-in kitchen scale weighs in steps of 1 gram up to 5 kg. The scale is easy to operate. It is ready for use when no operation is running.

**NOTES:**

- The starting weight is 2 grams.
- If you want to weigh 5 grams or less, inaccurate measurement results may occur.

### Simple weighing

1. The device is switched on, but no operation is running.
2. Press the *SCALE* button. The screen **7** shows the scale function and *0 g *.
3. Place the item to be weighed in the mixing bowl **5**. The weight up to 5 kilograms is displayed in grams on the screen** 7**.
4. If you add another ingredient to the mixing bowl **5**, the weight is added up in the display.
5. Press the arrow button *<* at the top left of the screen to end the scale function. The start menu appears again.

### Weighing with the tare function

With the tare function you can set the scale display back to `0` grams and then add new items to be weighed.

1. The device is switched on, but no operation is running.
2. Press the `SCALE` button. The screen **7** shows `0 g`.
3. Place the item to be weighed in the mixing bowl **5**. The weight up to 5 kilograms is displayed in grams on the screen** 7**.
4. Press `TARE` to set the weight display to `0 g`.
5. Add the new item to the one already there. The screen **7** now shows only the weight of the new item in grams.
6. If necessary, repeat the process with a new item.
7. Press the arrow button `<` at the top left of the screen to end the scale function. The start menu appears again.

# 4. Cooking programs

## 4.1 Kneading

**CAUTION!**

For firm and soft doughs the program may run for a maximum of 4 minutes; for liquid doughs a maximum of 6 minutes of uninterrupted run time is possible. After that: before using the Knead program again, please wait approx. 30 minutes. You can use other functions directly after kneading.

The program is operated only with the blade unit **18**.

| Heavy dough | Lighter, more liquid dough |
|---|---|
| max. 1,000 g flour for a maximum of 1,750 g dough | max. 1,000 g flour. Make sure that the dough mass does not exceed the **MAX** marking. |

### Guide values for the automatic kneading program

| Automatic program | Tool | Min. quantity | Max. quantity | Preset time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Firm dough (e.g. bread or pizza dough) | Blade unit **18** | 500 g dough | 1000 g flour / 1750 g dough | 2 min. |
| Soft dough (e.g. yeast dough or brioche) | Blade unit **18** | 500 g dough | 1000 g flour | 2 min. |
| Liquid batter (e.g. pancakes or crêpes) | Blade unit **18** | 500 g dough | 1000 g flour | 2 min. |

### General program information

- preset time: 2 minutes. (adjustable from 1 sec. - 4 or 6 min.)
- preset temperature: 0 °C (fixed)
- preset speed: 4 (fixed)
- forward/reverse run: controlled by the program

### Starting the program

1. Put the food into the mixing bowl **5**.
2. Close the mixing bowl **5** with the lid **23**.
3. The device is switched on and shows the program menu.
4. Press the `KNEAD` button.
5. 3 kneading programs appear on the left of the screen. One each for firm, soft and liquid dough.

**NOTE:** You can change the values for the time. Tap `TIME` at the bottom of the screen and make the desired setting.

6. Select the desired kneading program by tapping it.
7. Tap `START`. The set time is counted down on the screen **7**.
8. When the set time has elapsed, `DONE` appears on the right of the screen **7**.
9. Press the on/off switch **6** to switch the device off or go to the next recipe step.

**NOTE:** When processing yeast, please remember to dissolve it first in the lukewarm liquid (e.g. water or milk). Only then add the other ingredients.

## 4.2 Manual cooking

This program is not really a program at all, because here you make the specifications yourself. You set all the values yourself.

**NOTE:** It can happen that values you have set are changed automatically. This is always the case when your values conflict with other settings. Your settings are then adjusted automatically.

### Starting the program

1. Put the food into the mixing bowl **5**.
2. Close the mixing bowl **5** with the lid **23**.
3. The device is switched on and shows the program menu.
4. Press the `MANUAL COOKING` button. The values for the program appear on the left of the screen.

**NOTE:** You can enter all values *(`TEMPERATURE /`*

`TIME / LEVEL / DIRECTION OF ROTATION`) manually.

5. Tap the respective field on the left of the screen **7** and make the settings.
6. Tap `START`. The set time is counted down on the screen **7**.
7. When the set time has elapsed, a short beep sounds and `DONE` appears on the right of the screen **7**.
8. Carefully open the lid of the mixing bowl **5** and remove the food.
9. Press the on/off switch **6** to switch the device off or go to the next recipe step.

## 4.3 Searing

With this program you can lightly sear up to 500 g of meat, fish, vegetables and spices, e.g. to release roasted flavours. Large pieces must first be roughly chopped.

### General program information

- preset time: 7 min. (adjustable from 0 sec. - 14 min.)
- preset temperature: 130 °C (adjustable from 0 - 130 °C)
- preset speed: 1 (fixed)
- forward/reverse run: controlled by the program

### Starting the program

1. Put some fat (e.g. oil) into the mixing bowl **5**.
2. Put the food into the mixing bowl **5**.
3. Close the mixing bowl with the lid **23**.
4. The device is switched on and shows the program menu.
5. Press the `SEARING` button. The values appear on the left of the screen.

**NOTE:** You can change the values for the temperature and the time.

6. Tap the respective field on the left of the screen **7** and make the settings.
7. Tap `START`. The set time is counted down on the screen.
8. When the set time has elapsed, a short beep sounds and `DONE` appears on the right of the screen.
9. Press the on/off switch **6** to switch the device off or go to the next recipe step.

## 4.4 Steam cooking

**DANGER of injury from scalding!**

When opening the lid **1** or **23** during operation, hot steam may escape.

**NOTE:** When placing the food, make sure that some slots in the steamer insert **2** / **3** or in the cooking insert **19** remain free. Only then can the steam distribute freely and the ingredients are cooked evenly.

### General program information

- preset time: 20 min. (adjustable from 0 sec. - 60 min.)

- speed: `1`, not selectable
- After pressing `START`, a window appears in which you can choose whether the mixing bowl **5** should be preheated first. Select` YES`or` NO`.

Tips for steam cooking

Steam cooking is one of the healthiest ways to cook food. Not only are nutrients and colour of the food largely retained, but natural taste and bite also delight the diner. As a result, salt, fat and spices can be used sparingly.

### Using the steamer attachments

Depending on your needs, you can use either only the deep steamer attachment **3** or both steamer attachments (**2** and **3**). The shallow steamer attachment** 2 **cannot be used without the deep steamer attachment** 3**.

### Preparing menus

If you want to prepare a whole menu and therefore use both steamer attachments stacked on top of each other, please note:

- Meat and fish should always be cooked in the lower steamer attachment **3** so that dripping juices do not run over the other food.
- The food with the greatest weight should if possible be cooked in the lower steamer attachment **3**.

### Preparing the steamer attachments

1. Place the mixing bowl **5** in the seat **10**.
2. Pour 1 litre of water into the mixing bowl **5** (1.0L).
3. Check that the seal **4** of the deep steamer attachment **3** is seated correctly.
4. Place the deep steamer attachment **3** slightly twisted onto the mixing bowl **5**.
- Turn the deep steamer attachment **3** so that it clearly clicks into place and a beep sounds.
- The program can only be started when the steamer attachment **3** is correctly in place.
5. Put the food into the steamer attachment **3** and (if required) **2**.

**NOTE:** When placing the food, make sure that some slots in the steamer attachment **2** / **3** or in the cooking insert **19** remain free. Only then can the steam distribute freely and the ingredients are cooked evenly.

6. If you need it, place the filled steamer attachment **2** on the steamer attachment **3**.
7. Place the lid **1** on the top steamer attachment.

### Starting steam cooking

1. The device is switched on and shows the program menu.
2. Press the `STEAM COOKING` button. The values for the program appear on the left of the screen.

**NOTE:** You can change the values for the time. Tap `TIME` and make the setting.

3. Press the `START` button.
4. Choose whether or not the device should heat up (see section 4.4, General program information).
- `20 min` or the selected time is displayed in the time display on the left.
5. When the set time has elapsed, a short signal sounds and `DONE` appears on the right of the screen **7**.
6. Press the on/off switch **6** to switch the device off or go to the next recipe step.

**DANGER of injury from scalding!**

When opening the lid **1** or **23** during operation, hot steam may escape.

7. Remove the food from the steamer attachments **2**/**3**.

### Checking the doneness

You should take care not to cook the food for too long, as vegetables can otherwise become too soft and meat and fish too dry and tough.

**DANGER of injury from scalding!**

When opening the lid **1** during operation, hot steam may escape.

To check the condition of the food in between, proceed as follows:

1. Press `PAUSE` on the screen.
2. Carefully open the lid **1** for steamer attachments. When opening the lid **1**, make sure you are not hit by a gush of hot steam. Let the steam escape on the side facing away from you before opening the lid completely.
3. Check the condition of the food with a longer stick or a fork:
- If the food is done, press the `STOP` button.
- If the food is not yet done, put the lid back on and press the `RESUME` button.
4. To switch the device off, press the on/off switch **6** or go to the next recipe step.

### Overheating protection

The device has overheating protection. As soon as there is no more water in the mixing bowl **5** and the temperature therefore becomes too high, the device switches off automatically. In this case, pull the mains plug **15** and let the device cool down. After that you can put the device back into operation with water.

If the mixing bowl **5** becomes hot (60 °C) or even too hot, a window with the corresponding message appears on the screen **7** in each case. Press `OK` and, if necessary, follow the instructions on the screen. As long as the LED ring is flashing, the mixing bowl **5** is overheated.
